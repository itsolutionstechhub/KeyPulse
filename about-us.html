About Us

Welcome to KeyPulse, your premier web utility for computer input peripheral diagnostics. We build high-performance, web-based utilities that help gamers, developers, and writers troubleshoot their hardware in real-time, completely free of charge.

Our Mission

At KeyPulse, our mission is simple: to make hardware diagnostics easy and accessible to everyone. We believe that you shouldn't have to download third-party software or pay expensive service fees to test if your keyboard or mouse is working properly. By leveraging modern web standards, we provide zero-latency utility tools that run entirely inside your browser.

What We Offer

We provide two core testing tools, along with a comprehensive library of tech articles:

  • Interactive Keyboard Tester: An advanced console that displays real-time key registration, allowing you to test for key ghosting, keyboard chatter, and key response delay.
  • Mouse CPS Tester: A click speed utility designed to measure your Clicks Per Second (CPS) with real-time logs and ranking metrics.
  • Knowledge Base & Blogs: Expert maintenance and repair guides, ranging from fixing double-clicking switches to enabling laptop keyboard lights.

The Team

KeyPulse was developed and is maintained by the tech team at IT Solutions Pro. We are passionate about computer hardware, repairs, and software development, and we continuously update our platform to support new hardware features and web diagnostics.
