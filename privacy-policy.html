Privacy Policy

At KeyPulse, accessible from https://keypluse.online, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by KeyPulse and how we use it.

Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.

Information We Collect (Or Don't Collect)

No Input Logging: KeyPulse is built on a client-side architecture. This means all keyboard diagnostics, keystrokes, key latency details, and mouse clicks run entirely in your local browser window. We do not transmit, collect, save, or store any of your inputs or hardware testing data on any external servers.

Log Files

KeyPulse follows a standard procedure of using log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected by log files includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp, referring/exit pages, and possibly the number of clicks. These are not linked to any information that is personally identifiable. The purpose of the information is for analyzing trends, administering the site, tracking users' movement on the website, and gathering demographic information.

Google DoubleClick DART Cookie

Google is one of the third-party vendors on our site. It also uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to our site and other sites on the internet.

Advertising Partners Privacy Policies

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on KeyPulse, which are sent directly to users' browsers. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see on websites that you visit.

Note that KeyPulse has no access to or control over these cookies that are used by third-party advertisers.
